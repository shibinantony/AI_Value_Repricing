### Slide 3: The Threat from Frontier Labs
* **Header:** Front 1: The Model Commoditizers
* **Players:** OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, Palantir.
* **The Strategic Play:** Releasing headless agentic plugins (e.g., Claude Cowork, Palantir Foundry agents) that bypass application UIs.
* **The Boardroom Danger:** 
  1. Turning your core enterprise workflows into raw token consumption meters.
  2. Proprietary business rules trapped in vendor-specific prompt wrappers.
* **Architect's Rule:** Treat frontier LLMs as swappable utility commodities. Never hardcode logic into third-party prompts.

---

### Slide 4: The SaaS Seat Squeeze
* **Header:** Front 2: The SaaS Walled Gardens
* **Players:** Salesforce (Agentforce), ServiceNow (Otto), Workday, SAP.
* **The Commercial Dilemma:** If autonomous agents execute workflows via headless APIs, who pays $250/month per human seat?
* **The Vendor Countermove:** Pivoting from seats to "Agentic Work Units" and "Flex Credits."
* **The P&L Trap:** **Double Taxation.** Paying legacy per-user license fees *plus* metered consumption fees for every agent action.

---

### Slide 5: The GSI Margin Squeeze & Deflation
* **Header:** Front 3: The End of Headcount Arbitrage
* **Players:** HCLTech, TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant.
* **The Pressure Point:** **3–5% AI Deflation** on contract renewals as enterprise procurement demands 10–15% productivity pass-backs.
* **The Pivot:** GSIs are cannibalizing their own T&M pyramids to launch codified IP platforms (HCLTech AI Force, TCS WisdomNext, Infosys Topaz).
* **Enterprise Leverage:** 70% of Fortune 500 core architectures are 20+ years old. Black-box AI cannot connect to legacy cores without industrial SI integration.

### Slide 6: The Big 4 Diagnostic: Why Internal Pilots Stall
* **Header:** Strategy & Operating Model Failure Modes
* **EY:** Top-line adoption is vanity. Capitalizing reusable IP ("Earnings Quality") is the only hedge against deflation.
* **Accenture:** Enterprise models fail because they are organized around *tasks*. AI demands reorganizing around *decision rights*.
* **Deloitte:** 71% of enterprises now have 5+ tech chiefs (CIO, CTO, CDAO, CISO, CAIO). Internal turf wars freeze scaling.
* **PwC:** Bottom-up crowdsourced pilots create usage spikes but zero EBITDA impact.
