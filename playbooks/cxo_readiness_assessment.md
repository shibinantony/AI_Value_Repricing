## Assessment Instructions
Score each question from **1 (Non-Existent / Ad-Hoc)** to **5 (Fully Institutionalized / Automated)**.

```
Total Possible Points: 125
Maturity Tiers:
  • 25 – 50:  High-Risk Trap (Vulnerable to massive SaaS double-taxation & token bleed)
  • 51 – 75:  Fragmented Explorer (Siloed experimentation, low earnings quality)
  • 76 – 100: Transitioning Enterprise (Emerging control plane, partial IP capitalization)
  • 101 – 125: Sovereign Control Leader (Defensible IP moat, optimized FinOps, zero-trust agents)
```

---

### Dimension 1: Commercial Economics & Earnings Quality (CFO Mandate)
1. Are enterprise AI engineering investments capitalized on the balance sheet as reusable software assets rather than expensed as one-off consulting costs? (1–5)
2. Has enterprise procurement audited all major SaaS contracts (Salesforce, ServiceNow, SAP, Workday) to identify and eliminate "double taxation" (seats + consumption credits)? (1–5)
3. Do IT services Statements of Work (SOWs) enforce mandatory 10%–15% AI productivity pass-back rebates upon annual renewal? (1–5)
4. Are system integration contracts structured around fixed-outcome milestones rather than billable headcount pyramids? (1–5)
5. Does the enterprise legally own all custom domain ontologies, agent prompts, and orchestration workflows authored by external consultants? (1–5)

---

### Dimension 2: FinOps & Intelligence Arbitrage (CIO Mandate)
6. Does the enterprise operate an automated Model Abstraction Router that segregates routine tasks from complex reasoning? (1–5)
7. Is at least 70% to 80% of daily inference traffic processed by low-cost, fine-tuned Small Language Models (SLMs) rather than frontier models? (1–5)
8. Are automated, hard token-cost circuit breakers deployed to kill runaway agent loops? (1–5)
9. Can underlying foundation model providers (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral) be hot-swapped in less than 24 hours without breaking downstream business logic? (1–5)
10. Are inference costs attributed directly to business unit cost centers via real-time telemetry? (1–5)

---

### Dimension 3: Enterprise Context Graph & Institutional Moat (CTO Mandate)
11. Is corporate domain knowledge, regulatory compliance logic, and operational memory codified in an enterprise-owned Knowledge/Context Graph? (1–5)
12. Is retrieval augmented generation (RAG) powered by graph-based entity traversals rather than simple, noisy vector similarity? (1–5)
13. Are enterprise business rules completely decoupled from third-party vendor prompt templates? (1–5)
14. Does the enterprise maintain an automated synthetic evaluation suite to benchmark domain model accuracy continuously? (1–5)
15. Are core transactional workflows orchestrated independently of proprietary SaaS agent builders? (1–5)

---

### Dimension 4: Zero-Trust Governance & Runtime Assurance (CISO/CRO Mandate)
16. Is every autonomous AI agent registered and governed as a privileged Non-Human Identity with an assigned C-suite sponsor? (1–5)
17. Do agents operate under zero standing privileges with dynamically issued, ephemeral API tokens? (1–5)
18. Are hard financial and operational blast-radius circuit breakers (e.g., maximum transaction dollar caps) enforced at runtime? (1–5)
19. Does a secondary, independent "Auditor Agent" cross-verify state mutations before database commits in high-risk workflows? (1–5)
20. Does the AI operating environment continuously conform to ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, and MITRE ATLAS standards? (1–5)

---

### Dimension 5: C-Suite Alignment & GCC Transformation (CEO Mandate)
21. Has the enterprise formalized a unified Decision Rights Matrix (RACI) across the CIO, CTO, CDAO, CISO, and CAIO? (1–5)
22. Do Global Capability Centers (GCCs) operate as software IP foundries measured on reusable platform creation rather than headcount cost savings? (1–5)
23. Are autonomous agent workflows deployed in production across regulated core functions (Tax, Treasury, Claims, Cyber)? (1–5)
24. Is human oversight structured around exception handling and policy definition rather than manual task execution? (1–5)
25. Does the Board of Directors receive quarterly reporting on AI earnings quality, IP capitalization value, and agentic risk telemetry? (1–5)
